About Recife

Recife is a city located on the Atlantic coast of northeastern Brazil. It is the capital of the state of Pernambuco.

  • State: Pernambuco
  • Language: Portuguese
  • Currency: Real brasileiro (R$)
  • Population: ~1,7 million
  • Area: 218.84km²
