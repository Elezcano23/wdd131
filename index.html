About Me

My name is Ezequiel Lezcano. I was born in Choele Choel, Río Negro province, but I currently live in the city of 9 de Julio, Buenos Aires province. I currently work as an auditor and data analyst. I'm a saxophonist and I play in the band "Altimus Club."

9 de Julio, Argentina

Argentina is a large South American country with a landscape that includes the Andes Mountains, glacial lakes, and the grasslands of the Pampas, the traditional grazing land of its famous cattle. The country is known for the dance and music of tango. Its large, cosmopolitan capital, Buenos Aires, centers around the Plaza de Mayo, surrounded by imposing 19th-century buildings, such as the Casa Rosada, the iconic presidential palace.
